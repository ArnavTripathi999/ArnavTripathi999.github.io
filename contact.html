Arnav Tripathi
aka SMooKER_999

3D artist, animator and Unity developer based in Delhi NCR, India, and co-founder of NoteX Studios.

I started out as a gamer who wanted to know how horror games get under your skin. That curiosity turned into modelling and animating in Blender, then building gameplay in Unity with C#. Today I make games and game assets with the NoteX Studios team. Our first title, Phantom Echoes: Chapter I, is out on Steam, and The Last Peg is on the way.

When I'm not working, I'm probably playing something that keeps me up at night.
