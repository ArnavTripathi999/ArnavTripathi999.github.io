The game

Can you uncover the truth behind the echoes?

Phantom Echoes: Chapter I is a first-person psychological horror game built around exploration, puzzle-solving and survival. It's billed as India's first multiverse horror title.

You play as Ronny, trapped in a hotel where the environment seems distorted, sounds echo unnaturally and reality itself feels unstable. As you explore, you uncover fragments of a disturbing story while trying to work out what is real.
