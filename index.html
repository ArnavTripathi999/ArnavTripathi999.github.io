01 · Intro

Hi, I'm Arnav.
I make the dark look good.

I'm a 3D artist, animator and Unity developer from Delhi NCR, and a co-founder of NoteX Studios, an independent studio making story-driven games and game assets.

Most of what I make lives somewhere between atmosphere and gameplay: creepy hotel corridors, creatures that shouldn't exist and the lighting that makes you not want to turn the next corner. I model and animate in Blender, then bring it all to life in Unity with C#. Our first release, Phantom Echoes: Chapter I, is out now on Steam.

02 · My backstory

How it started

Every horror story has an origin. Mine starts with a controller, gets weird in Blender and ends up on Steam.

Chapter 01

Player one

It started as a gamer. Somewhere between late-night horror runs I stopped just playing and started wondering how those worlds were built, lit and timed to make you jump.

Chapter 02

First polygons

I opened Blender and started modelling: props first, then creatures and full environments. Rigging and animation followed, because a monster that doesn't move isn't scary.

Chapter 03

Making it playable

Art needed rules, so I picked up Unity and C#: interactions, puzzles, AI that hunts you down and the lighting and audio cues that hold it all together.

Chapter 04

NoteX Studios

I co-founded NoteX Studios in New Delhi: a young indie studio with a six-person team and partners around the world. Our motto: "We say it, we mean it."

May 2026

Phantom Echoes ships

Our first game, Phantom Echoes: Chapter I, launched on Steam: a first-person psychological horror billed as India's first multiverse horror title.

Now

What's next

Working on The Last Peg, building assets for clients through Asset on Demand, and turning other people's game ideas into playable builds.
