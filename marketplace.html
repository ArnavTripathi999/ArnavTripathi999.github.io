FAQ

Questions

Which engines do you support?

We work in Unity. Assets come in standard formats (.fbx, .glb), so they also work in most other engines.

Who owns the work?

You do. For Game on Demand you get full rights to publish and sell the game. For Asset on Demand the assets are yours to use in your projects.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the job. A single prop can take days; a full game takes longer. You'll get a timeline with your quote.

Can you do horror?

It's our favourite genre. Check out Phantom Echoes.
